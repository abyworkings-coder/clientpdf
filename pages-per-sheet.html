    Compositing pages, not just shrinking them

    Pages per Sheet lays 2 or 4 original pages onto each printed sheet using pdf-lib's embedPages/drawPage — genuinely different from cropping or resizing, since it composites multiple source pages into a brand-new page rather than trimming or rescaling existing ones. Choose a landscape or portrait sheet layout; each source page is scaled and centered within its cell automatically, saving paper and ink for anything you're printing rather than reading on screen.

    Compositing multiple full pages into each output page means the tool is effectively re-rendering the whole document — for a long or image-heavy source PDF, that's more work for the browser to do at once than a straightforward page reorder.
