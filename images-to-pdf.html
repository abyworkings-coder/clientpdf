    Turning a batch of photos or scans into one document

    Images to PDF takes any number of JPG or PNG files, lets you reorder them before conversion, and lays out one image per page at its native size — no forced cropping or stretching. It's the inverse operation of Extract Images, and useful for turning a folder of phone-scanned receipts or a stack of photographed whiteboard pages into a single file you can actually send someone.

    Because each image becomes a full embedded page rather than a re-compressed thumbnail, a batch of a few dozen full-resolution photos can produce a genuinely large PDF, and building it entirely in the browser tab (no server queue to offload to) takes real memory.
